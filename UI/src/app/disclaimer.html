Radionics Disclaimer

Radionics is, in its essence, a spiritual art. This application is provided for personal spiritual practice, contemplation, and experimental exploration. It is not a substitute for medical analysis, diagnosis, professional advice, or treatment.

Your health and the target’s health are your responsibility

You are responsible for your own health decisions and for the decisions and actions you take toward any person or animal you designate as a “target.” Do not transfer that responsibility to this application, its results, a rate list, a broadcast, or a practitioner. If you use the app on behalf of another person, you are responsible for acting with care, obtaining appropriate consent, and ensuring that your actions do not interfere with their access to qualified care. Their own choices and rights must be respected.

Personal responsibility includes recognizing the limits of your knowledge and seeking appropriate help. It does not make you qualified to diagnose or treat another person, and it does not remove the duties of healthcare professionals, guardians, or caregivers.

No medical diagnosis or treatment

The app does not examine the body, perform laboratory tests, assess symptoms, prescribe medicines, or provide medical or veterinary services. Its use of words such as “analysis,” “general vitality” (GV), “rate,” “energy,” “resonance,” and “broadcast” describes functions within a spiritual and experimental practice. These terms must not be interpreted as clinical measurements or medical findings.

A high or low GV, a ranked rate, a resonance event, or a completed broadcast does not establish the presence or absence of disease, identify its cause, demonstrate recovery, or prove that a treatment works. Never use these outputs to decide whether someone is healthy, safe, or in need of medical help.

Continue appropriate professional care

Do not replace, postpone, or avoid medical assessment or treatment because of radionics or anything shown by this app. Do not stop or change prescribed medication, treatment, follow-up appointments, or a clinician’s instructions on the basis of app results. Discuss health concerns and any proposed changes in care with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional. For an animal, consult a veterinarian.

If you or your target may be experiencing a medical emergency, seek emergency assistance immediately. Do not run an analysis, wait for a broadcast to finish, or repeat GV checks before seeking urgent care.

Experimental outputs and no promise of benefit

Scores and resonance events are generated by software using experimental camera-derived randomness or, during broadcasting when needed, unseeded random numbers. They are not measurements of physical energy, biological function, or a target’s health. Animations and broadcast durations do not show that an effect has been transmitted to a target. The app provides no clinically validated diagnostic or treatment result, and no healing, prevention, improvement, or other outcome is promised.

Spiritual meaning, personal impressions, and perceived changes are subjective. A meaningful experience does not by itself establish a medical effect or a causal connection with an illness or recovery.

Consent, vulnerable targets, and respectful use

Respect the privacy, autonomy, and beliefs of any target. Obtain informed consent where appropriate before using another person’s name, image, or personal information. Do not use radionics to pressure someone into changing treatment or accepting spiritual beliefs. When a target is a child, a dependent person, or an animal, responsible guardianship and access to appropriate professional care remain essential.

Use with care

Stop using the app if its visual effects cause discomfort, dizziness, distress, or other adverse symptoms. Do not use it while driving or operating machinery. Its screens, notifications, and spiritual interpretations must never distract you from an immediate health or safety need.

You remain responsible for how you interpret and act on the app’s outputs. Treat them as material for spiritual reflection and experimentation, and make health decisions with qualified professional support.
